AI & BusinessCurrent Work

AI + Business Workflow

Applying AI to recurring business tasks — not as a chat interface, but as part of how the work gets done.

Problem

Recurring business tasks — reports, analysis, information processing — take real time and pull attention away from higher-value work.

Approach

I use AI as part of my own workflow to draft and structure reports, summarise information and support business analysis, rather than doing every step manually.

Technology

General-purpose AI tools applied directly to business workflows — a way of working, not a standalone product.

Status

In active use as part of my own recurring business workflow.

What I learned

The value isn't AI replacing the work — it's AI removing the mechanical parts so the judgment calls get more of my attention.
🤖
AI AgentsCurrent Work

AI Agents for Business Operations

Moving AI from a tool you ask questions to a system that helps run recurring operations.

Problem

Recurring administrative and bookkeeping work is repetitive but still needs judgment — a rigid script breaks easily, a fully manual process doesn't scale.

Approach

Developing AI-agent-based workflows that assist with business administration and bookkeeping — a step beyond a single AI query, toward something that participates in the recurring process.

Technology

AI agents applied to administrative and bookkeeping workflows.

Status

In development, with the scope of automation deliberately bounded by human review.

What I learned

Agents are a genuinely different design problem from chatbots — the hard part is deciding what they're allowed to do without a human checking every step.

Experiments & ToolsOpen Data

HK Shadow
陰涼路線

A shade-aware walking route finder for Hong Kong, built on government open data — not an AI project, an open-data and mapping one.

Problem

Hong Kong's summer heat makes route choice matter — the fastest walking route and the most comfortable one are often different, and there was no easy way to see both.

Approach

Built a browser-based route planner that calculates two routes side by side — fastest and shadiest — instead of forcing a single answer.

Technology

Hong Kong government open data (buildings, footpaths), live weather data and browser-based mapping — no backend, no accounts, no AI/ML.

Status

Live: a free, working tool in the Apps section of this site — open-data engineering shipped end to end.

What I learned

Small, well-scoped open-data projects are a fast way to test how "possible" becomes "practical" — the hard part is rarely the algorithm, it's the data.
