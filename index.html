What I believe

Technology is only valuable when it works in the real world. The gap between what technology can do and what organisations actually need is where much of the real work begins — and it's the gap I'm interested in, across AI, drones and emerging technologies meeting business, engineering, operations and society.

Technology→Business→Operations→Society

01 — AI Capability

What the technology can actually do today.

02 — Business Need

What the organisation actually needs to achieve.

03 — Operational Reality

How the work actually gets done, day to day.

04 — Practical Application

Where capability, need and reality can actually meet.

05 — Business Value

The outcome that makes the whole exercise worthwhile.

I call this the Applied AI Gap — a way I think about technology and business problems, not a formal framework.
